# Key Lime Pudding Parfaits
Florida‑bright, creamy, make‑ahead, and perfect for coastal lunches.

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Chill Time:** 2 hours  
**Servings:** 6  
**Make Ahead:** Yes — they’re better after chilling.

These parfaits are cold, refreshing, and effortless. They layer key‑lime pudding with whipped cream and a buttery graham crumble. They hold up beautifully in the fridge and won’t melt or collapse, even in Florida heat.

---

## Ingredients

### Key Lime Pudding
- 1 box instant vanilla pudding mix (3.4 oz)  
- 2 cups cold milk  
- ⅓ cup key lime juice  
- Zest of 1 lime  

### Graham Crumble
- 1 cup graham cracker crumbs  
- 2 tbsp melted butter  
- 1 tbsp sugar (optional)

### Topping
- Whipped cream  
- Extra lime zest  
- Thin lime slices (optional)

---

## Steps

1. **Make the pudding:**  
   Whisk pudding mix with cold milk until thickened. Add key lime juice and lime zest. Chill 10 minutes.

2. **Make the crumble:**  
   Mix graham crumbs, melted butter, and sugar until sandy.

3. **Assemble parfaits:**
