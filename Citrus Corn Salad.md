# COASTAL COOKOUT MENU  
## Citrus Corn Salad  
Charred corn, cotija cheese & a bright lime-orange dressing  

**Prep Time:** 15 min  
**Grill Time:** 10 min  
**Servings:** 6  
**Make Ahead:** Yes — perfect for prepping early.

Sweet corn charred right on the grill, tossed with juicy cherry tomatoes, snappy red onion, creamy cotija, and a citrus dressing with just enough cumin and chili to keep things interesting.

---

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 6 ears fresh corn, husked  
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved  
- ½ red onion, finely diced  
- ¾ cup cotija cheese, crumbled (or feta)  
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped  
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely minced (optional)  
- Lime wedges for serving  

### Citrus Dressing
- 3 tbsp fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)  
- 1 tbsp fresh orange juice  
- ½ tsp orange zest  
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil  
- ½ tsp ground cumin  
- ¼ tsp chili powder  

---

## Steps

1. Grill the corn until charred, about 10 minutes.  
2. Cut kernels off the cob.  
3. Toss corn with tomatoes, onion, cotija, cilantro, and jalapeño.  
4. Whisk dressing ingredients together.  
5. Pour dressing over salad and toss.  
6. Serve with lime wedges.
