Citrus Corn Salad
Charred corn, cotija cheese & a bright lime-orange dressing
PREP TIME
15 min
GRILL TIME
10 min
SERVINGS
6
MAKE AHEAD
Yes!
This is summer in a bowl. Sweet corn charred right on the grill, tossed with juicy cherry tomatoes, snappy red onion, creamy cotija, and a citrus dressing with just enough cumin and chili to keep things interesting. It tastes like a Florida farmers market and a taco stand had the best possible summer romance.
INGREDIENTS
SALAD
	•	6 ears fresh corn, husked 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved ½ red onion, finely diced ¾ cup cotija cheese, crumbled (or feta) ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely minced (optional) Lime wedges for serving
CITRUS DRESSING
	•	3 tbsp fresh lime juice (about 2 limes) 1 tbsp fresh orange juice ½ tsp orange zest 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil ½ tsp ground cumin ¼ tsp chili powder Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
INSTRUCTIONS
🌙 NIGHT-BEFORE PREP
	1	Grill corn: heat grill to high. Place husked ears directly on grates and cook 10–12 minutes, turning every 2–3 minutes, until charred in spots. (Oven method: roast at 425°F for 20 minutes, turning once.) Let corn cool completely, then stand each ear upright in a large bowl and cut kernels from the cob with a sharp knife. You'll have about 4 cups of charred kernels. Make citrus dressing: whisk together lime juice, orange juice, orange zest, olive oil, cumin, and chili powder. Season with salt and pepper. Dice red onion, halve cherry tomatoes, and mince jalapeño if using. Store corn kernels, prepped vegetables, and dressing separately in covered containers in the refrigerator overnight.
☀️ DAY-OF INSTRUCTIONS
	1	Remove corn and vegetables from the refrigerator 15 minutes before serving. Combine corn kernels, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and jalapeño (if using) in a large bowl. Pour citrus dressing over the salad and toss well to coat. Taste and adjust — add more lime juice, salt, or a pinch more chili powder to your liking. Fold in fresh cilantro and crumbled cotija just before serving, reserving a little of each for the top. Transfer to a serving bowl. Top with reserved cotija, a dusting of chili powder, and a few cilantro leaves. Serve with lime wedges on the side.
🌊 Coastal Tip: No jalapeño? Add a pinch of cayenne to the dressing for a subtle background heat without the peppers. The charred corn is the star — don't skip the grilling step. That smoky flavor makes this salad unforgettable.
